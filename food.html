Lebanese Food

Discover the delicious flavors and traditional dishes of Lebanon.

Introduction to Lebanese Cuisine

Lebanese cuisine is an important part of Lebanon's culture and everyday life. It is known for its fresh ingredients, herbs, olive oil, vegetables, grains, and a variety of spices. Lebanese meals are often shared with family and friends, making food an important part of social gatherings.

Lebanese cuisine includes many vegetarian dishes as well as meat and seafood. Popular ingredients include chickpeas, eggplant, garlic, lemon, mint, parsley, olive oil, and tahini.

Popular Lebanese Dishes

Hummus

Hummus is a popular Middle Eastern dish made mainly from chickpeas, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and olive oil. It is commonly served as part of a Lebanese mezze.

Tabbouleh

Tabbouleh is a fresh salad made with parsley, tomatoes, bulgur, mint, lemon juice, and olive oil. It is one of the most recognizable dishes of Lebanese cuisine.

Kibbeh

Kibbeh is traditionally prepared with bulgur, minced meat, onions, and spices. It can be prepared in different shapes and cooking styles, depending on the region and recipe.

Manakish

Manakish is a popular Lebanese flatbread. It can be topped with za'atar, cheese, or other ingredients and is commonly eaten for breakfast.

Lebanese Mezze

Mezze is a style of eating in which many small dishes are served together. A Lebanese table may include hummus, tabbouleh, fattoush, olives, pickles, cheese, and other dishes. Mezze is often shared by everyone at the table and is an important part of Lebanese hospitality.

How a Lebanese Meal Can Begin

  1. Fresh bread is placed on the table.
  2. Small mezze dishes such as hummus and tabbouleh are served.
  3. Additional dishes such as kibbeh or grilled meats may follow.
  4. The meal can finish with Lebanese sweets and coffee.

Lebanese Food and Culture

Food in Lebanon is more than just something to eat. Preparing and sharing food is an important part of family life and hospitality. During celebrations and gatherings, families often prepare several dishes so that everyone can enjoy a variety of flavors.
