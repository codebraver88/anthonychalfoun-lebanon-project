About Lebanon

A Country on the Mediterranean

Lebanon is a small country located on the eastern coast of the Mediterranean Sea. It is known for its beautiful mountains, Mediterranean coastline, ancient cities, and rich cultural heritage.

Quick Facts About Lebanon

Fact Information
Capital Beirut
Official Language Arabic
Currency Lebanese Pound
Location Eastern Mediterranean
Coastline Mediterranean Sea

What Makes Lebanon Special?

Lebanon has a long history and a diverse culture. Visitors can explore ancient ruins, lively cities, mountain villages, beaches, forests, and traditional markets.

  • Beautiful Mediterranean coastline
  • Lebanon Mountains
  • Ancient archaeological sites
  • Rich music and cultural traditions
  • Famous Lebanese cuisine

Popular Places to Discover

Lebanon has many interesting destinations. Some famous examples include Beirut, Byblos, Baalbek, the Cedars of Lebanon, and the Chouf region.

  1. Beirut
  2. Byblos
  3. Baalbek
  4. The Cedars of Lebanon
  5. Chouf
