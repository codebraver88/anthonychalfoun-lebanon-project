Lebanese Traditions & Culture

Explore some of the traditions, music, celebrations, clothing, and everyday lifestyle of Lebanon.

Lebanese Culture

Family and Hospitality

Family is an important part of Lebanese society. Lebanese hospitality is also well known, and guests are often welcomed warmly and offered food and drinks.

Traditional Music and Dance

Music is an important part of Lebanese culture. One famous traditional dance is the Dabke. It is often performed by groups of people who hold hands and dance together to rhythmic music.

  • Dabke
  • Traditional Arabic music
  • Drums and percussion
  • Traditional singing

Lebanese Celebrations

Lebanese communities celebrate many religious, national, and cultural occasions throughout the year. Celebrations often bring families and communities together through food, music, dancing, and gatherings.

Village and Cultural Festivals

During summer, many Lebanese towns and villages organize festivals featuring music, performances, local food, and cultural activities.

Traditional Clothing

Traditional Lebanese clothing has changed over time. Historical Lebanese clothing could vary depending on the region and community. Today, modern clothing is common, while traditional outfits may still appear during cultural events and performances.

Everyday Lebanese Lifestyle

Lebanese lifestyle combines modern city life with strong family connections and traditions. Cafés, restaurants, markets, family gatherings, and outdoor activities are important parts of social life.

  1. Spending time with family and friends
  2. Sharing meals together
  3. Visiting cafés and restaurants
  4. Enjoying the mountains and Mediterranean coast
  5. Taking part in cultural celebrations
